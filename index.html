HOWORX Publishing Client

An internal tool for preparing and uploading the HOWORX channel operator's engineering explainers. The client is not a public sign-up service and is not sold or distributed to other creators.

What the client does

  1. The authorized channel operator selects a finished video and reviews its title, description, visibility and synthetic-media disclosure.
  2. With the operator's Google authorization, the client uploads the video using YouTube API Services.
  3. The client records the upload ID and checks processing and visibility so it can report the result without creating duplicate uploads.

The local desktop/command-line client is available only to its operator. There is no public login or demo account. Reviewers may request a supervised demonstration using the contact address below.
